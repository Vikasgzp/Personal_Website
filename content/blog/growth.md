Six semesters ago, my goal wasn't a 9+ SGPA.  
It was simply to **pass**.

I still remember asking my classmates how they managed to score well, while I was hoping for just one or two extra marks to clear a few subjects. Back then, even imagining a 9+ semester felt unrealistic.

Today, I received my 6th semester result:

# **𝟗.𝟏𝟎 𝐒𝐆𝐏𝐀 🎯**

What makes me happier, though, isn't the number itself.

It's knowing **how far the journey has been.**

---

## 📈 The Journey

From **𝟔.𝟒𝟔 𝐒𝐆𝐏𝐀 𝐢𝐧 𝐦𝐲 𝐟𝐢𝐫𝐬𝐭 𝐬𝐞𝐦𝐞𝐬𝐭𝐞𝐫** to a current **𝐒𝐆𝐏𝐀 𝐨𝐟 𝟗.𝟏𝟎 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐂𝐆𝐏𝐀 𝐨𝐟 𝟖.𝟐𝟎**, this didn't happen because of one exceptional semester. It happened because of many ordinary days of trying to do a little better than before.

There's something else that made me smile today.

This semester, I missed so many **𝐒 𝐠𝐫𝐚𝐝𝐞𝐬** by just 2–3 marks.

A few years ago, I was hoping for just 1–2 extra marks to reach 40 and pass a few subjects.

It's interesting how the same few marks can mean completely different things at different stages of life.

---

## Where You Begin Doesn't Define Where You Finish

This result isn't a destination, and I don't see it as a major achievement. It's simply another reminder that **where you begin doesn't have to decide where you finish.**

I think that's the lesson I'll carry forward.

Whenever I face something difficult—whether it's placements, learning a new technology, or any challenge in the future—I want to remember that I've been in this position before.

I started my JEE preparation from a small local coaching institute in difficult circumstances, and I started college struggling academically. Neither of those starting points defined the outcome.

---

## Progress Is Often Gradual

Sometimes progress is so gradual that you don't notice it while it's happening.

Until one day, you look back and realize you've become the person who once seemed impossibly far ahead.

---

## Grateful for the People Along the Way ❤️

Finally, I'm grateful to a few special friends whose presence made more difference than they probably realize.

Sometimes the best motivation isn't constant advice—it's simply being surrounded by people who inspire you to raise your own standards.

Thank you for being part of this journey.
